How you show up

Be where students already plan their future.

Career Launch partnerships run on an annual term. Your content launches in April and works for the full school year, then renews. The April 2027 launch is Ontario-focused; if you operate elsewhere in Canada, there's a national option.

Everywhere else

Paid LaunchPad contributor

LaunchPad is myBlueprint's free career video hub, open to students right across the country. Contribute content now to reach students nationally, and get first call when Career Launch goes Canada-wide in 2028. See it at launchpad.myblueprint.ca.

Directory coverage is agreed province by province. If you're an association looking to put your members on the record, that's a separate conversation and we should have it.

A note for post-secondary partners: you're welcome as a content partner, and several joined us in 2025. What we don't carry on a student-facing surface is an admissions funnel — no application links, inquiry forms or "request info" buttons. Career storytelling, yes; recruitment of a fifteen-year-old, no.
