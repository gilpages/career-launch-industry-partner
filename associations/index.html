How it works

Your members participate. Your industry benefits. We make it easy.

You are an advocate for your industry, not a reseller, and we will not ask you to act like one. Your members hear about this from you, and we take every onboarding and partnership conversation directly.

1 · Your association

Tell your members

Introduce it through your newsletter, your member list, your events and your industry channels, with your association’s name on it. We take every conversation from there.

No selling. No administration.

2 · Your members

Give members a new benefit

Two things to put in one email: a free verified listing in the national employer directory, open to every member, and an optional paid content partnership for members who want to go further.

Each member decides for itself.

3 · Back to you

Show your industry’s impact

How many of your members took part, how many schools and school boards they reached, and at which grade levels. Evidence that your association mobilised its sector, for a board report or a grant application.

Once a year, in writing.

4 · What it costs

Nothing

No fee to your association, and no charge to members for a directory listing. Content partnerships are optional and arranged directly with us. Nothing here needs a budget line.

Nothing to fund.

Two offers to your members

One is free and open to every member. The other is optional and paid.

Most associations introduce both in the same email: a free offer every member can take up, and a paid option for members who want to go further. Members deal with us on both.

Free for every member

A verified directory listing

A member adds its organization and verifies it: its sites, its seasons, and what it will actually do with a student. Verified records rank above unverified ones, and the date it verified stays on the record. Verified means the organization itself confirmed the record, on the date shown.

  • Co-op placements
  • Apprenticeships, OYAP and RTA ready
  • Sector-Partnered Experiences
  • Guest speaker, in person
  • Guest speaker, virtual
  • Field trip facilitation
  • Certification training
  • Volunteer opportunities
  • French-language friendly

Who it suits. Members with premises and staff who are willing to have a school get in touch. A member picks what it offers, can offer nothing but visibility, and can change it at any time.

Optional and paid

A Career Launch content partnership

A member’s own people on screen, telling students what the work is like, credited to the member and framed by myBlueprint. It runs on LaunchPad, on the member’s sector page, and inside the collections teachers assign to a whole class.

Members arrange this with us directly. Your association does not quote it, sell it or administer it.

Who it suits. Members who want students to know the industry exists, whether or not they are set up to host one. This does not depend on a directory listing and works for a membership that is not built to take students on site.

What neither of them is. We do not source, match or fill placements, and a directory listing is not a recruitment guarantee. What a member gets is to be findable by the schools nearest it, with a record of what it is willing to do. The ask still comes from the teacher.
